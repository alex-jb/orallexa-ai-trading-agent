# Research and competition opportunities (checked 2026-10-01 UTC)

Orallexa's defensible contribution is a reproducible evaluation and audit trail for **paper-only** financial agents. It has not established trading alpha. Draft work on fixed-rule paper execution, signed snapshots, synthetic fault injection, and a predeclared 90-pair evaluation should be reported with the exact code, permitted data, seeds, costs, and failure cases. A test with synthetic events says nothing about Alpaca fills or live-market profitability. Do not submit a performance table while the full data cohort remains unavailable or unevaluated.

An engineering audit alone may be outside it without a real methodological ML contribution. Neither route justifies inventing market data or calling paper fills live evidence.
