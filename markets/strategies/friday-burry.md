# Friday — Michael Burry · 逆向 + 资产负债表 + 看 footnotes

**Weekday:** 4 (Friday)
**Core thesis:** "Knowledge is hard to come by, real edge comes from understanding things others miss in the footnotes."

## Framework

For each top mover, do the contrarian read:

1. **What's the consensus narrative?** — what do retail / sell-side / Twitter all believe? Write it in one sentence.
2. **What's the contrarian read?** — what evidence does the consensus narrative ignore?
3. **Balance sheet check:**
   - Cash on hand vs 12-month burn
   - Debt maturity wall — is there a refinance cliff in next 18 months?
   - Hidden liabilities (pension shortfalls, operating leases, warrants outstanding)
   - Working capital trend — improving or deteriorating?
4. **Footnotes / SEC filings deep dive:**
   - Recent 10-Q/10-K vs prior period — what changed in accounting policy?
   - Stock-based compensation as % of revenue (if > 20%, real earnings are lower)
   - Related-party transactions (red flag if growing)
5. **Asymmetric short setup** (Burry's specialty):
   - If business model has structural flaw consensus is ignoring, can you short with limited downside (deep OTM puts, defined-risk spreads)?

## Burry-specific signals

- ✅ Lean in on: overvalued darlings with deteriorating fundamentals masked by hype
- ✅ Lean in on: overlooked value plays with strong cash, hated narrative, mean reversion ahead
- ❌ Avoid: anything where you only have the consensus thesis and no counter-evidence
- ⚠ Burry is wrong ~40% of the time but his hits are 5-10x. Sizing matters more than win rate.
