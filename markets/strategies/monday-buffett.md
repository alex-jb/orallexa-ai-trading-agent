# Monday — Warren Buffett · 护城河 + 复利 + 不亏钱

**Weekday:** 0 (Monday)
**Core thesis:** "Rule #1: never lose money. Rule #2: never forget rule #1."

## Framework (apply to today's top 3 movers)

For each ticker, answer in 2-3 sentences:

1. **Moat / 护城河** — does this company have a structural advantage (brand, network effect, switching cost, low-cost producer, regulatory) that lasts 10+ years? If you can't name the moat in one sentence, it's not a moat.
2. **Owner-earnings quality** — is the GAAP earnings backed by real free cash flow, or is it accrual-heavy / capex-deferred? Quick proxy: FCF/net-income ratio over 3 years.
3. **Management with skin in the game** — does insider ownership ≥ 5%? Are they buying or selling on the open market?
4. **Margin of safety** — current price vs your conservative 10-year DCF. If you can't get 30% margin of safety, pass.

## Buffett-specific filters

- ❌ Skip if: no consistent 10-year ROE > 15%, debt/equity > 2x, business model you can't explain to your grandmother
- ✅ Lean in if: durable moat, owner-operator, returns capital to shareholders (buybacks > dividends preferred), simple business

## Quote to anchor

> "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre."
