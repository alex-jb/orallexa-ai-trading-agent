# Thursday — George Soros · 反身性 (Reflexivity) + 短线宏观

**Weekday:** 3 (Thursday)
**Core thesis:** "Markets are constantly in a state of uncertainty and flux. Fundamentals don't determine prices; **prices change fundamentals**."

## Framework — Reflexivity loops

For each top mover, identify if there's a reflexivity feedback loop in play:

1. **Self-reinforcing trend?** — is the price action ITSELF improving the fundamentals?
   - Stock up → easier to raise capital → more growth → stock up more (boom)
   - Stock down → harder to refinance → forced selling → stock down more (bust)
2. **Narrative phase** — Soros 8-stage boom/bust:
   1. Unrecognized trend
   2. Beginning of self-reinforcing process
   3. Successful test
   4. Growing conviction → faster acceleration
   5. Reality lag / divergence
   6. Twilight zone (peak)
   7. Crossover (reality breaks)
   8. Catastrophic decline
3. **Where are we on the curve?** — stage 3-4 is the trade. Stage 6-7 is the dangerous one.

## Soros-specific signals

- ✅ Lean in at stages 3-4 (early enough that narrative is forming, late enough to confirm direction)
- ❌ Avoid stages 6-7 (twilight zone) — fundamentals diverging but story still seductive
- ✅ "When you see a bubble forming, the rational thing to do is to buy that bubble, as Soros pointed out"
- ⚠ "It's not whether you're right or wrong that's important, but how much money you make when right and how much you lose when wrong"
