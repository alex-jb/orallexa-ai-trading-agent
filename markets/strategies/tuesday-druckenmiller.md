# Tuesday — Stanley Druckenmiller · 宏观 top-down + 押注 concentrated

**Weekday:** 1 (Tuesday)
**Core thesis:** "It's not whether you're right or wrong, but how much money you make when you're right and how much you lose when you're wrong."

## Framework

For today's top 3 movers, apply this checklist:

1. **Macro tailwind** — what's the bigger economic / policy / liquidity wave this ticker is riding? (Fed pivot? AI capex cycle? Reshoring? Defense spending? Energy transition?). No macro tail = pass.
2. **Catalyst within 12 months** — is there a specific event (earnings, FDA, regulation, IPO) that forces re-rating?
3. **Asymmetric payoff** — if you're right, can it 3-5x? If wrong, can you stop out at -15%? (3:1 minimum R:R)
4. **Position size by conviction** — Druckenmiller goes 20%+ on highest-conviction. Most traders should cap at 5%, but the concept matters: don't size by tradition, size by conviction × asymmetry.

## Druckenmiller-specific signals

- ✅ Lean in when: macro and micro align (good company in good sector in good macro)
- ✅ Cut fast: "When I'm wrong, the only thing I know to do is be right the next time by not losing money."
- ❌ Skip diversification for diversification's sake — too many small positions = noise

## Quote to anchor

> "If you make a lot of money in the next bull market — and you let everybody take it away from you in the next bear market — that's not investing."
