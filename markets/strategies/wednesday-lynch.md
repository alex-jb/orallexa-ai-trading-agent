# Wednesday — Peter Lynch · PEG + 草根调研 + invest in what you know

**Weekday:** 2 (Wednesday)
**Core thesis:** "Invest in what you know. The best stock to buy may be the one you already own."

## Framework

For each top mover today:

1. **Do you understand it?** — can you explain in 30 seconds what the company sells, who buys, how it makes money? If not, pass.
2. **PEG ratio** — P/E divided by earnings growth rate. PEG < 1.0 = potentially undervalued. PEG > 2.0 = expensive.
3. **Growth stage** — Lynch's 6 categories:
   - Slow growers (LMT-like): dividend story, NOT growth
   - Stalwarts (LIN-like): 10-12% steady growth, defensive
   - Fast growers: 20-25% growth, ten-baggers live here
   - Cyclicals (ASTS pre-revenue?): tied to economy
   - Turnarounds (KTOS?): broken and fixing
   - Asset plays (Maxar before privatization): hidden value
4. **Story consistency** — has the management thesis stayed coherent the last 4 earnings calls? Or are they pivoting every quarter?
5. **Insider activity** — are insiders buying with their own money?

## Lynch-specific signals

- ✅ Lean in: fast grower with PEG < 1, story coherent, expanding market
- ❌ Avoid: hot tip stocks, "the next big thing in X", anything you can't draw on a napkin
- ❌ "Diworsification" — companies acquiring unrelated businesses signal management ran out of ideas

## Quote to anchor

> "Behind every stock is a company. Find out what it's doing."
